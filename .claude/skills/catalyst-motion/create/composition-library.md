# Composition library — Type 1 layouts

Most references use one composition: **a central monolith with satellites and rising pillars**. Reusing it is
the fastest way to make new work look copied. Choose deliberately.

| Archetype | Shape | Best for | Light flows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monolith** *(reference default — use sparingly)* | One hero block centred, satellites around it | A single product identity | Up and out from the hero |
| **Pipeline** | 3–5 solids in a row along one iso axis, rising slightly | CI/CD, ETL, workflows, hand-offs | Along the axis, left-low → right-high |
| **Terraces** | Stepped plateaus, each higher than the last | Tiers, plans, maturity, scaling | Climbs the steps |
| **Constellation** | 5–9 small nodes scattered on the grid, no hero, linked by traces | Networks, events, distributed systems | Node to node, in many directions |
| **Hub & ring** | A hub at the centre, a ring of small solids or pillars around it on the floor | Schedulers, brokers, orchestration | Radial, clockwise sequence |
| **Strata** | Layers stacked vertically, separated by gaps | Full-stack, abstraction layers, OSI-like | Vertical, through the layers |
| **Twin** | Two solids apart, a gap or bridge between them | Integration, migration, sync, before/after | Across the gap |
| **Field** | A grid of identical cells on the floor, no solids taller than 0.3 units | Data sets, tables, indexes, ML features | Scan sweeps, cells lighting up |
| **Gate** | A thin frame across a lane | Security, validation, limits | Through the frame, some stopped |
| **Tower** | A tall, thin stack seen from low in the frame, filling the height | Capacity, compute, growth | Up the inside, beam-like |

## Framing rules (all archetypes)
- **Screen placement is not fixed.** Where the art sits and how big it is (close-up, mid, panorama) comes from the scene's frame layout in `frame-layouts.md`. Don't default to art on the right and text on the left: that is layout L1, used at most once per film.
- Keep the **dominant iso diagonal readable.** Main flow runs either left-low → right-high, or top-down through the centre.
- **Negative space at least 35%.** It's the black void from the DNA.
- **Glows:** use 2 large, soft ellipses behind the art, one teal-led and one ember-led. Place them at opposite ends of the main flow so the gradient is read *as* the direction of travel.

## Mixing archetypes for films
- Across the scenes of one film, **use a different archetype per Type 1 scene**. Repeating one feels like a template.
- A reprise (CTA scene) may return to the film's first archetype at ≤ 40% opacity to close the loop.
